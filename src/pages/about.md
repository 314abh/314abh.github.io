Hi, I'm **314abh**. I'm a low-level software engineer &mdash; the kind who is
happiest reading a disassembly listing, staring at a memory map, or chasing a
heisenbug through a device driver.

This site is my public notebook. Posts tend to be about:

- operating systems and the kernel/userspace boundary,
- compilers, linkers, and the toolchain that stitches it all together,
- embedded and systems programming in C and Rust,
- the small performance details that only matter when they really matter.

## Colophon

This site is deliberately tiny. It is built with [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/)
and served as static HTML. The design is intentionally retro: warm paper tones,
hard shadow borders, and a terminal-flavoured typeface for headings, because I
grew up on the web of hand-written pages and miss it.

- **No build-time JavaScript is shipped to you** beyond a few lines that
  remember your light/dark preference.
- **No fonts or scripts are fetched from third parties.**
- Math is rendered ahead of time with KaTeX, so even the equations cost you
  nothing at runtime.
