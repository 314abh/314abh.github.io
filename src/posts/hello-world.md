Every project starts with a `hello, world`. This is the one for this site.

I have spent most of my working life close to the metal &mdash; allocators,
interrupt handlers, cache lines, linker scripts, the kind of code where a
stray pointer is not a stack trace but a very quiet machine. I have filled
countless scratch files and notes with things I learned and then forgot. This
is an attempt to keep some of that in one place.

So: a blog. Hand-rolled, static, and small enough to fit in your head. It is
generated by [Eleventy](https://www.11ty.dev/) from plain Markdown and served
as flat HTML. No framework, no client-side router, no bundle to parse before
you can read a paragraph.

A few principles for whatever ends up here:

- **Text first.** Pages should be readable on a decade-old laptop, an e-reader,
  or a phone on a bad connection.
- **No tracking.** There are no cookies, no analytics scripts, no fonts fetched
  from a third party. The only JavaScript is a few lines to remember your
  light/dark preference.
- **Corrections over deletion.** If I get something wrong, I would rather fix it
  in the open than quietly memory-hole the post.

If you got here from somewhere else on the web: welcome. There is an
[RSS feed](/feed.xml) if you would like to follow along in a reader.
