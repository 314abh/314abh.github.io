It's a matter of grief to me. Even a little discomfort .. realizing
the sheer number of people that don’t realize that any given local
optimum is not necessarily, and in fact almost always, never the
global optimum. So much could’ve been simplified, and an immense
number of arguments and discrimination could’ve just never existed.

As a student of computer science, I’ve been actively looking for
jobs. I must find work. I see a baffling quantity of people at high
positions who have no idea what they’re doing. Surprisingly, they
aren’t even scared that one day it might be exposed that they’re not
competent enough for their position. Yet, they will lecture hundreds
if not thousands of people about what _they_ should do in life. I
think that happens -- the false courage -- can come only when they’ve
assumed a global optimum in the infinite-dimensional function of
life. One must always remember: **local optimum is almost never the
global optimum**.
